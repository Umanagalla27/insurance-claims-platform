# VOYAGER GLOBETROTTER INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL PROTECTION PLAN (TRV-2026)

## Policy Overview
Voyager International Assistance Underwriters provides travel insurance coverage for the trip dates and geographical destination countries specified on the Certificate of Insurance.

---

## Section 1: Covered Travel Benefits

### 1.1 Trip Cancellation and Interruption
Reimburses 100% of non-refundable prepaid travel expenses (flights, hotel deposits, cruise fares, tour bookings) if the trip must be canceled or cut short due to:
- Sickness, accidental injury, or death of the Insured, a Traveling Companion, or an immediate Family Member.
- Inclement weather causing complete cessation of commercial air travel for at least 24 consecutive hours.
- A documented strike or mechanical breakdown of a common carrier.
- Subpoena, jury duty service, or mandatory quarantine order.

### 1.2 Baggage Loss, Damage, and Baggage Delay
- **Lost Baggage**: Reimburses up to $2,500 for permanent loss or theft of checked luggage by a common carrier, subject to a $500 per-item limit.
- **Baggage Delay**: Reimburses up to $500 for the emergency purchase of necessary personal toiletries, clothing, and medication if checked baggage is delayed by the airline for more than **twelve (12) consecutive hours**.

### 1.3 Emergency Medical Expenses and Medical Evacuation
- **Emergency Medical**: Up to $100,000 for emergency medical treatment and hospitalization abroad resulting from sudden illness or injury occurring while traveling.
- **Emergency Evacuation**: Up to $250,000 for emergency air ambulance transport to the nearest adequate medical facility or repatriation to the home country.

---

## Section 2: General Travel Exclusions

### 2.1 Pre-Existing Medical Conditions
Excludes claims arising from medical conditions for which medical advice, diagnosis, care, or treatment was recommended or received within the **sixty (60) days** prior to the policy effective date, unless the Pre-Existing Condition Waiver was purchased within 14 days of initial trip deposit.

### 2.2 Hazardous Activities and Adventure Sports
Injuries or evacuation costs incurred while participating in skydiving, hang gliding, bungee jumping, scuba diving below 30 meters, mountaineering requiring ropes, or professional competitive sports are excluded.

### 2.3 Government Sanctions and Travel Advisories
Excludes travel to nations subject to official United Nations or US Department of State Level 4 Travel Advisories ("Do Not Travel") prior to departure.

---

## Section 3: Claims and Documentation Requirements

- **Police and Carrier Reports**: Baggage theft must be reported to local police within 24 hours. Airline luggage delays require an official Property Irregularity Report (PIR).
- **Submission Window**: All travel insurance claims must be filed within **ninety (90) calendar days** of the scheduled trip completion date with original boarding passes and receipts.
