# GUARDIAN HERITAGE 20-YEAR LEVEL TERM LIFE CONTRACT (TL-2026)

## Contract Structure
Guardian Heritage Life Assurance Company promises to pay the Face Amount (Death Benefit) designated in the Policy Schedule to the designated Beneficiary upon receipt of due proof of death of the Insured during the active policy term.

---

## Section 1: Death Benefit & Payout Provisions

### 1.1 Face Amount Settlement
Upon satisfactory proof of death (Certified Copy of Death Certificate and completed Claimant Statement), the Insurer disburses the full Face Amount in a single lump sum or structured settlement annuity.
- **Grace Period**: A grace period of thirty-one (31) days is allowed for payment of renewal premiums, during which full death coverage remains in force.

### 1.2 Accelerated Death Benefit Rider
If the Insured is diagnosed by a licensed medical practitioner with a qualifying terminal illness with a life expectancy of twelve (12) months or fewer, the policyholder may request an advance of up to 75% of the policy Face Amount (maximum $500,000). The remaining death benefit payable to beneficiaries is reduced proportionately.

---

## Section 2: Exclusions and Incontestability

### 2.1 Two-Year Incontestability Period
The validity of this policy shall not be contested after it has been in force during the lifetime of the Insured for **two (2) years from its Issue Date**, except for non-payment of premiums or fraudulent material misrepresentations made in the application.

### 2.2 Suicide Exclusion Clause
If the Insured commits suicide, whether sane or insane, within **twenty-four (24) months** from the Issue Date, the Insurer's total liability is limited strictly to a refund of all premiums paid, less any policy indebtedness. Full face value is payable after the 24-month suicide exclusion window expires.

### 2.3 Aviation Exclusion
Death resulting from piloting, operating, or serving as a crew member on an aircraft (other than as a fare-paying passenger on a scheduled commercial airline) is excluded unless an explicit Aviation Rider is attached.

---

## Section 3: Beneficiary Designation and Rights

- **Revocable vs Irrevocable**: The policyholder may change a revocable beneficiary at any time without the beneficiary's consent.
- **Contingent Beneficiary**: If the primary beneficiary predeceases the Insured, benefits pass automatically to designated contingent beneficiaries.
- **Filing Deadline**: Claims for life death benefits should be filed within **ninety (90) days** of death or as soon as reasonably possible.
