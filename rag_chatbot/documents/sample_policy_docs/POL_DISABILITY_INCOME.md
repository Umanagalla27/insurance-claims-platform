# PINNACLE EXECUTIVE LONG-TERM DISABILITY CONTRACT (LTD-2026)

## Policy Overview
Pinnacle Financial & Life Assurance issues this Group Long-Term Disability Policy to provide monthly income protection for eligible full-time employees unable to work due to qualifying sickness or injury.

---

## Section 1: Disability Definitions and Benefit Schedules

### 1.1 Total Disability Definition
- **Own Occupation Period (First 24 Months)**: The Insured is considered Totally Disabled if sickness or injury prevents them from performing the substantial and material duties of their regular "Own Occupation", and they are under the regular care of a licensed physician.
- **Any Occupation Period (After 24 Months)**: Following 24 consecutive months of benefit payments, the Insured is considered Totally Disabled only if unable to perform the duties of "Any Gainful Occupation" for which they are reasonably qualified by education, training, or experience.

### 1.2 Monthly Benefit Calculation
- **Benefit Percentage**: 60% of pre-disability basic monthly earnings, subject to a maximum monthly benefit cap of $10,000.
- **Offsets**: Benefits are integrated with and reduced by other income benefits, including Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI), Workers' Compensation payments, and state disability benefits.

### 1.3 Elimination Period
Benefits begin only after the expiration of a mandatory **ninety (90) consecutive day Elimination Period** from the date of initial disability onset, during which no benefits are payable.

---

## Section 2: Special Limitations and Exclusions

### 2.1 Mental and Nervous Disorder Limitation
Monthly disability benefits for disabilities caused or contributed to by mental disorders, psychiatric illness, depression, anxiety, or stress are capped at a lifetime cumulative maximum of **twenty-four (24) months**.

### 2.2 Pre-Existing Condition Exclusion (3/12 Rule)
Disabilities manifesting within the first twelve (12) months of coverage are excluded if caused by a condition for which medical consultation, tests, or prescription drugs were received in the three (3) months immediately prior to the effective date.

### 2.3 Excluded Causes of Disability
No disability benefits are payable for disabilities resulting from:
- War, whether declared or undeclared, or acts of foreign aggression.
- Active participation in a riot, rebellion, or insurrection.
- Intentionally self-inflicted injuries or suicide attempts.
- Committing or attempting to commit a felony.

---

## Section 3: Notice of Claim and Ongoing Proof

- **Initial Notice**: Written notice of disability claim must be submitted within **thirty (30) days** after the date disability starts, or as soon as medically practicable.
- **Continued Proof**: Ongoing medical certification of total disability and financial documentation of earnings must be submitted every six (6) months upon request.
