# SECUREGUARD PREFERRED AUTO POLICY CONTRACT (AP-2026-X)

## Policy Overview
This Personal Auto Policy establishes an agreement between SecureGuard Casualty ("the Insurer") and the Named Insured designated on the Declarations Page. Coverage applies strictly during the active policy term within the United States, its territories, and Canada.

---

## Section 1: Coverage A — Liability to Others

### 1.1 Bodily Injury and Property Damage
The Insurer agrees to pay compensatory damages for which any Insured becomes legally liable due to an automobile accident resulting in bodily injury or property damage.
- **Split Limits of Liability**: Bodily injury coverage is capped at $100,000 per person, $300,000 per occurrence. Property damage liability is capped at $50,000 per occurrence.
- **Legal Defense**: The Insurer will defend any civil suit brought against the Insured seeking damages covered by this policy, even if allegations are groundless, false, or fraudulent. The Insurer's obligation to defend terminates when applicable policy limits are exhausted through settlement or judgment.

---

## Section 2: Coverage B — Physical Damage to Covered Auto

### 2.1 Collision Coverage
The Insurer will pay for direct, accidental physical damage to the Insured Vehicle caused by an upset or impact with another vehicle or object.
- **Standard Deductible**: A mandatory deductible of $500 applies per collision event.
- **Valuation Basis**: Payouts are calculated on an Actual Cash Value (ACV) basis minus depreciation, not replacement cost.

### 2.2 Comprehensive (Other-Than-Collision) Coverage
Covers physical damage to the covered vehicle resulting from perils other than collision:
- Fire, explosion, earthquake, windstorm, hail, flood, or water damage.
- Vandalism, malicious mischief, riot, or civil commotion.
- Contact with a bird or animal.
- Breakage of glass.
- Theft or attempted larceny of the vehicle.
- **Comprehensive Deductible**: $250 per occurrence.

---

## Section 3: General Exclusions — What We Do Not Cover

### 3.1 Commercial and Ride-Sharing Exclusion (TNC)
No coverage is provided under any section of this policy while the vehicle is logged into a Transportation Network Company (e.g., Uber, Lyft) or digital delivery platform, or while transporting passengers or goods for retail compensation, unless an explicit Rideshare Endorsement is purchased.

### 3.2 Intentional Acts and Criminal Conduct
The Insurer provides zero indemnification for bodily injury or physical damage caused intentionally by or at the direction of the Insured, or occurring during the commission of a felony or while evading law enforcement.

### 3.3 Unlicensed or Excluded Drivers
No coverage applies if the vehicle is operated by any individual specifically listed on the Named Driver Exclusion Endorsement, or by any driver lacking a valid state driver's license.

### 3.4 Racing and Demolition Contests
Damages sustained while participating in, preparing for, or practicing for any prearranged or organized racing, speed, or stunt contest on a closed track or public roadway are entirely excluded.

---

## Section 4: Claims Procedures and Mandatory Deadlines

### 4.1 Notice of Accident or Loss
The Insured must provide prompt formal notice of any accident or loss to the Insurer within **thirty (30) calendar days** of the incident. Notice must include date, time, location, involved parties, police report reference number, and contact details of witnesses.

### 4.2 Preservation of Evidence and Vehicle Inspection
The Insured must take reasonable precautions to protect the damaged vehicle from further loss. The Insurer reserves the right to inspect the vehicle prior to the commencement of any non-emergency repairs.
