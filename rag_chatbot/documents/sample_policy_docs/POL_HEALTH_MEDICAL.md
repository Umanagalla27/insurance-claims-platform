# APEX PREMIER COMPREHENSIVE HEALTH BENEFIT PLAN (MED-2026-B)

## Plan Summary
This Group Health Benefit Plan is administered by Apex Healthcare Management Corporation. Benefits are provided for medically necessary healthcare services subject to plan deductibles, co-insurance, co-payments, and network provisions.

---

## Section 1: Inpatient and Outpatient Covered Services

### 1.1 Inpatient Hospitalization
- Semi-private room and board, intensive care unit (ICU) charges, general nursing care, and hospital meals.
- Operating room, recovery room, and surgical suite fees.
- Diagnostic lab tests, pathology, and diagnostic imaging performed during inpatient confinement.
- **Inpatient Co-insurance**: 80% paid by plan, 20% member responsibility after annual deductible.

### 1.2 Outpatient Ambulatory Surgery
- Medically necessary surgical procedures performed in an accredited hospital outpatient department or freestanding ambulatory surgical center.
- Pre-admission laboratory testing conducted within 7 days prior to scheduled procedure.

### 1.3 Prescription Drug Formulary
- **Tier 1 (Generic Drugs)**: $15 copay per 30-day retail supply.
- **Tier 2 (Preferred Brand Drugs)**: $45 copay per 30-day supply.
- **Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Specialty Drugs)**: 30% co-insurance up to maximum $250 out-of-pocket per prescription. Specialty medications require specialty pharmacy fulfillment.

---

## Section 2: Prior Authorization Requirements

### 2.1 Mandatory Prior Authorization
The following services strictly require approved Prior Authorization from Apex Medical Review before service delivery, except in life-threatening medical emergencies:
- Inpatient hospital admissions and extended acute rehabilitation stays.
- Elective outpatient spine, orthopedic, and cardiovascular surgeries.
- High-technology radiology scans: MRI, CT, and PET scans.
- Specialty injectable biologics and oncology infusion treatments.
- **Penalty for Failure to Authorize**: Retrospective authorization failure results in a 50% benefit reduction up to a $1,500 penalty.

---

## Section 3: General Plan Exclusions

### 3.1 Cosmetic and Elective Procedures
Surgeries and treatments performed primarily for aesthetic enhancement, psychological well-being, or modification of bodily appearance without underlying structural or functional impairment are excluded. Breast reconstruction following mastectomy is covered under the Women's Health and Cancer Rights Act (WHCRA).

### 3.2 Experimental, Investigational, and Unproven Treatments
Medical devices, pharmaceuticals, surgical techniques, or therapies not established as standard-of-care by the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) or prevailing clinical guidelines are excluded.

### 3.3 Out-of-Network Non-Emergency Care
Services rendered by non-participating healthcare providers or unaccredited facilities without prior network exception approval are excluded from benefit calculation.

### 3.4 Work-Related Injuries and Illnesses
Any injury or disease arising out of and in the course of employment covered under state Workers' Compensation statutes is excluded from plan coverage.

---

## Section 4: Cost-Sharing, Deductibles, and Out-of-Pocket Maximums

- **Individual In-Network Annual Deductible**: $1,500 per calendar year.
- **Family In-Network Annual Deductible**: $3,000 per calendar year.
- **Maximum Out-of-Pocket (MOOP)**: $6,500 individual / $13,000 family. Once met, eligible in-network covered expenses are paid at 100% for the remainder of the plan year.
- **Claim Filing Deadline**: Out-of-pocket reimbursement claims must be submitted with original itemized HCFA-1500 or UB-04 bill forms within **one hundred eighty (180) days** of service date.
