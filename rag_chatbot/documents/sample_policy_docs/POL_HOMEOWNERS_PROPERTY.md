# KEYSTONE HOMEOWNERS BROAD FORM PROPERTY POLICY (HO-3-2026)

## Policy Overview
Keystone Mutual Insurance Company provides property insurance for the single-family residence described on the Declarations Page.

---

## Section 1: Property Coverages

### 1.1 Coverage A — Dwelling
Covers the primary residential building located on the residence premises, including attached structures, fixtures, and permanently installed heating, cooling, and plumbing systems.
- **Valuation**: Covered on a Replacement Cost Value (RCV) basis, provided the dwelling is insured to at least 80% of its full replacement value.

### 1.2 Coverage B — Other Structures
Covers detached structures on the residence premises set apart by clear space (e.g., detached garages, fences, sheds, and swimming pools). The standard limit is 10% of the Coverage A dwelling limit.

### 1.3 Coverage C — Personal Property
Covers household contents, furniture, clothing, and personal belongings owned or used by an Insured anywhere in the world.
- **Special Sub-limits**:
  - Jewelry, watches, and furs: $1,500 maximum for loss by theft.
  - Firearms and related equipment: $2,500 maximum.
  - Silverware, goldware, and pewterware: $2,500 maximum for loss by theft.
  - Electronic data processing equipment and computers: $5,000 maximum.

### 1.4 Coverage D — Loss of Use (Additional Living Expenses)
If a covered peril makes the residence premises uninhabitable, the Insurer covers necessary increase in living expenses incurred by the household to maintain its normal standard of living for up to twelve (12) consecutive months.

---

## Section 2: Covered Perils (Insured Events)

### 2.1 Fire, Lightning, and Internal Explosion
Direct physical loss caused by sudden, accidental fire, smoke from sudden malfunctions, or atmospheric electrical discharge.

### 2.2 Burst Plumbing and Accidental Water Discharge
Covers sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system, or from within a household appliance.
- **Sudden and Accidental Rule**: Does not cover damage occurring gradually over a period of weeks, months, or years due to undetected seepage.

---

## Section 3: Exclusions — Losses Not Covered

### 3.1 Flood, Surface Water, and Storm Surge
Excludes any loss caused directly or indirectly by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of any body of water, or spray from any of these, whether driven by wind or not. Flood protection requires a separate National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policy.

### 3.2 Sewer and Drain Backup
Damage resulting from water or water-borne material backing up through sewers, drains, or discharged from sumps or septic systems is excluded unless the Sewer and Sump Backup Endorsement (HO-0208) is attached.

### 3.3 Earth Movement and Soil Subsidence
Excludes earthquake, landslide, mudflow, sinkhole collapse, subsidence, or earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

### 3.4 Wear, Tear, Marring, and Gradual Deterioration
Excludes normal wear and tear, rot, mold, rust, corrosion, decay, settling, cracking of foundations, vermin infestation, or neglect by the property owner to maintain structural integrity.

---

## Section 4: Deductibles and Conditions

### 4.1 Base Policy Deductible
All property loss settlements are subject to a mandatory deductible of $1,000 per claim occurrence, deducted from total eligible adjusted loss.

### 4.2 Proof of Loss and Claim Submission
The Insured must submit a sworn proof of loss statement within **sixty (60) days** of the Insurer's request, itemizing damaged property, repair estimates, purchase receipts, and photographic evidence.
