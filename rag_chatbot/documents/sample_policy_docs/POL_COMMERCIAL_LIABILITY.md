# VANGUARD COMMERCIAL GENERAL LIABILITY POLICY (CGL-2026)

## Policy Overview
Vanguard Underwriters issues this Commercial General Liability Coverage Form to the Named Insured business entity identified in the Declarations.

---

## Section 1: Coverages

### 1.1 Coverage A — Bodily Injury and Property Damage Liability
The Insurer will pay those sums that the Insured becomes legally obligated to pay as damages because of bodily injury or property damage to third parties caused by an occurrence within the coverage territory:
- **Premises and Operations**: Accidents occurring on the commercial business premises or resulting from ongoing business operations.
- **Products and Completed Operations**: Liability arising from manufactured goods sold, handled, or distributed, or completed construction/service contracts once work has been relinquished to the customer.

### 1.2 Coverage B — Personal and Advertising Injury Liability
Protects against legal liability resulting from false arrest, malicious prosecution, wrongful eviction, libel, slander, disparagement, oral or written publication violating privacy rights, or trademark/copyright infringement in advertising materials.

---

## Section 2: Exclusions

### 2.1 Expected or Intended Injury
Bodily injury or property damage expected or intended from the standpoint of the Insured, except reasonable force used solely to protect persons or property.

### 2.2 Liquor Liability Exclusion
Applies only to businesses engaged in manufacturing, distributing, selling, or serving alcoholic beverages. Liability resulting from causing or contributing to intoxication or serving minors is excluded.

### 2.3 Workers' Compensation and Employer's Liability
Any obligation of the Insured under a workers' compensation, disability benefits, or unemployment compensation law, or bodily injury claims by an employee arising out of employment.

### 2.4 Professional Liability and Errors & Omissions
Excludes claims arising from the rendering of or failure to render professional architectural, engineering, accounting, medical, or legal services. Requires a separate Professional Liability (E&O) policy.

### 2.5 Pollution and Environmental Contamination
Total exclusion for bodily injury or property damage arising out of the actual, alleged, or threatened discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants at any time.

---

## Section 3: Limits of Insurance and Defense

- **General Aggregate Limit**: $2,000,000 per policy term.
- **Products-Completed Operations Aggregate Limit**: $2,000,000.
- **Each Occurrence Limit**: $1,000,000 maximum payout per single covered accident or event.
- **Duty to Defend**: Legal defense costs are provided outside policy limits until the occurrence limit is reached.
- **Notice of Claim**: The Insured must provide written notice of an occurrence, offense, claim, or legal suit **as soon as practicable** within thirty (30) business days.
